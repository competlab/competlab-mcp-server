# Example Prompts

Once CompetLab MCP is connected, try these prompts with your AI agent. All prompts work with Claude, Cursor, VS Code Copilot, and other MCP clients.

## Getting Started

> "List my CompetLab projects"

> "Show me the project details and when each dimension was last monitored"

> "Who are the competitors I'm tracking?"

## AI Visibility

> "Which companies do ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews recommend in my category — and am I one of them?"

> "Where do I stand on the AI market map, and which companies make up its core?"

> "What's my AI Visibility Score and how does it compare to competitors?"

> "How has the AI market map moved over the last 3 months — who is recommended more often, and who less?"

> "Show the AI visibility trend for Perplexity alone — is it naming us more often than at the start of the window?"

> "Which competitor is named in the most AI answers — and is that true for every engine, or just one?"

## AI Sources

> "Which pages do Perplexity and Google AI Overviews read for my buyers' questions that name my competitors but not me?"

> "Give me the AI Sources work list — the pages worth getting onto, with the evidence"

> "Did an engine open one of our own pages and still recommend someone else?"

## Strategic Briefing

> "Show me the strategic briefing — what should I fix first?"

> "What are the top competitive threats I should address this week?"

> "Give me the full strategic briefing — pull the competitor and pricing sections"

> "What did last month's briefing say, and what has changed since?"

## Pricing Intelligence

> "What changed on my competitors' pricing pages recently?"

> "Compare pricing plans across all competitors — who offers what at each tier?"

> "Show me critical pricing alerts from the last 7 days"

> "Pull the pricing gap analysis — where are we over/under-priced?"

## Content Intelligence

> "Compare content strategies across all tracked competitors"

> "What new URLs did competitors add to their sitemaps this week?"

> "Show me content changes for competitor X — filter by blog posts only"

> "Which competitor has the most docs/landing pages?"

> "Which competitors publish templated (programmatic) pages at scale?"

## Positioning

> "How do competitors position themselves on their homepages?"

> "Compare our value proposition vs the top 3 competitors"

> "What CTAs are competitors using? Any patterns?"

## Tech & Trust

> "Which competitors have better security headers than us?"

> "Compare trust signals — who has the most compliance badges?"

> "Which AI assistants can reach each competitor's site, according to their robots.txt?"

> "What tech stack changes have competitors made recently?"

## Alerts & Monitoring

> "What alerts fired in the last 7 days?"

> "Show me all critical alerts across all dimensions"

> "What's our monitoring schedule? When is the next run?"

> "Filter alerts for just pricing changes from competitor X"

## Free Tools (no project setup required)

These work on any public domain — no `projectId` needed. Sync tools return immediately; the async scans return a `scanId` you can poll.

> "Run a sitemap check on stripe.com and tell me what content categories they have"

> "Which AI assistants can fetch openai.com's pages, according to its robots.txt?"

> "Compare AI-crawler policies for openai.com, anthropic.com, and google.com"

> "Detect the tech stack on vercel.com — what frameworks, analytics, and CMS are they using?"

> "Start a trust-signals scan on linear.app and poll until it completes — then summarize their enterprise readiness"

> "Run an Agent Adoption Check on my domain — what's blocking AI agents from working with my site?"

> "Run sitemap, tech-stack, and trust-signals scans on competitor X in parallel, then summarize what they're doing better than us"

## Reports & Analysis

> "Build me a competitive briefing I can share with the team"

> "Summarize all 6 monitored dimensions for our main competitor"

> "Create a weekly competitive digest with the most important changes"

> "What are the biggest gaps between us and the market leader?"
